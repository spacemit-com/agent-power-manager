# Skill Card: Meeting Notes

## Capability

Separates decisions from action items in meeting notes.

## Use Cases

- Recording a brief meeting note.

## Inputs and Outputs

- Input: decisions and action items from a meeting.
- Output: two separate bullet lists, one per kind.

## Dependencies

- None.

## Limitations

- Keeps decisions and actions apart; it does not summarize the discussion.
